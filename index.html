    About Debatekeeper

    Debatekeeper times speeches in debates and rings bells automatically at the correct times. It supports most parliamentary styles, including British Parliamentary, Australs, World Schools, Asian Parliamentary, American Parliamentary and Canadian Parliamentary[cite: 2].

    The app rings a bell, vibrates and/or flashes the screen white (or any combination of the three) at bell times. It also rings configurable overtime bells[cite: 2].

    Using the timer

    Press Start to begin a speech. Slide left or right across the timer screen to switch between speeches. Bells ring at the times set by the debate style, and the screen colour changes to show what is happening: green when points of information are allowed, orange after a warning bell, and red once the speech is over time. Stop to pause, Resume to continue. When POIs are allowed, the POI button starts a 15 second countdown. Keyboard: Space starts and stops, and the left and right arrow keys change speech[cite: 2].
